## Top Pieces

### Transformer & Performer (from scratch)
Originally my A-level NEA project, I continue to work on this (dubbed "Gavin") throughout my academic experience as I learnt new tools and approaches, especially with recent advancements in Large Language Models.

An implementation of [transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) and [performer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14794) architectures from first principles to understand attention mechanics and performance tradeoffs.

- Focus: attention math, efficiency, training workflow
- Stack: Python, Keras (TensorFlow), C++ for data Processing + Speed
- Repo: https://github.com/Gavin-Development

### Better PSO
A Particle Swarm Optimization visualizer that makes the algorithm's behavior easy to inspect, explain, and teach.
Made during my years at university to assist in my own learning, and future students at [University of Kent](https://kent.ac.uk)

- Focus: optimization intuition, visualization
- Stack: C++, ImGui & CMake
- Repo: https://github.com/Scot-Survivor/Better-PSO
